Settings

How often Kai's helper tries to reach Kai while Kai is not running. When the helper itself cannot start, the extension waits this long before it starts it again, doubling the wait with each failure in a row up to one minute.

Firefox's Back and Forward do nothing, silently, when there is no such page. Kai's Back and Forward report that when Firefox has not started a navigation within this time.

How long Kai's Open, Go, Back, Forward and Reload wait for Firefox to load the page. After this time Kai is told the page has not loaded yet; the tab goes on loading.

How long Kai waits for a page (each of its frames) to answer when it reads the page or one of its elements. A page whose own script keeps it busy cannot answer; after this time Kai is told so.

How long Kai may press keys to fill one field: a slider stepped toward its number, a suggestion field typed one letter at a time. When the time is up Kai stops and is told how far it got.
